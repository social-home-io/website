[`mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/`](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/).

letterlijke tekst — bijvoorbeeld
[`social-home-io/socialhome/LICENSE`](https://github.com/social-home-io/socialhome/blob/main/LICENSE).
