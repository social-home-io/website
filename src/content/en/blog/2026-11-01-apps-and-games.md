Social Home now has **Apps**: games and small tools your household
adds once, for everyone at home. The first five are **Chess**,
**Go**, **Battleships**, **Backgammon** and **Gift draw**.

Play with someone at home, with a friend in a paired household, or start
a game in a [space](/docs/glossary/#space) and let whoever is free join
in. And just in time for December, the gift draw lets the whole family
draw names for Secret Santa, even when they live in different homes,
and nobody can peek.

It's all in **Social Home 2026.11.1**.

_The screens below show three sample families: the Müllers, the Okafors
and the Krämers._

## The Apps page

Tap **Apps** in the menu. **Your apps** shows what your household
already has. The **Store** shows everything you can add, with a short
note on what each app can do and how many can play.

An admin adds an app once, and everyone at home can use it. Would you
like one? Tap **Ask an admin**. Admins can also set a minimum age, or
switch an app off for a while without losing anything saved in it.

**Apps can't snoop.** An app never sees your password, posts, chats,
photos or where you are, and it can't go on the internet. It only
reaches the people you choose to play with. Children's protected
accounts only see apps for their age, and only play with people they can
already message.

## Four games to play together

Each game is the real thing, with all the rules, on your phone or a big
screen. Take your time: a game can run for days. Social Home lets you
know when it's your turn, and new invites wait for you under **Needs
you**.

**Chess.** Choose your colour and invite someone. Games where it's your
move are listed first.

## Games in a space

A space can now have its own games. The people who look after the space
pick which games are allowed. Until they do, there are none.

Then anyone in the space can tap **Start a game**, add a note like
"Another round, anyone? Beginners welcome", and wait for someone to
join. **Let everyone know** sends the whole space a quiet heads-up.

## Gift draw: Secret Santa that nobody can peek at

Everyone draws one name and gets a present for that person. Normally
somebody ends up knowing the whole list. With **Gift draw**, nobody
does: not the person who set it up, not the app, not anyone else.

**Set it up.** Give it a name, a budget and the day you swap presents,
and write your wish list. Then invite people. One tap picks everyone in
a space, across all their homes. Up to 30 people can take part.

**Keep couples apart.** Add pairs who shouldn't draw each other, then
tap **Draw names**.

between them, so the full list is never in one place.

Someone drops out after the draw? The person who drew them gets the
name they had drawn, and everyone else keeps theirs. You can still update your
wish list afterwards, and the person buying for you sees it.

One honest note: in a very small draw, with everyone in one or two
homes, a home's own server may be able to work out who drew whom. The app
tells you when that's the case.

<details class="tech">
<summary>Under the hood</summary>

- **Apps** run in a sandboxed frame with no network and no access to
  Social Home's API; they talk to Social Home only through a bridge.
  Each app declares what it needs (`storage`, `people`, `federation`),
  and an update that asks for more waits for an admin. The app list
  comes from Social Home; a server operator can change it.
- **Game moves** between two households travel encrypted home to home
  over the pairing you already have, not through a GFS. Both sides check
  every move. Battleships seals every square when you lock in (salted
  SHA-256); Backgammon dice come from two hash chains exchanged before
  play.
- **Group games** (2 to 30 seats) are hosted by the household of
  whoever started them. A space game reaches only the space's member
  households, never its followers or a GFS.
- **Gift draw** is a decryption mix: each household wraps its people's
  entries in layers, the households shuffle in turn, and each card is
  sealed to its giver (X25519, HKDF-SHA256, AES-256-GCM, signed with
  Ed25519). A household that cheats in the shuffle is named to everyone
  and a fresh round follows; a household can still force a few redraws
  without being named, and after 3 the draw stops.
- **What it doesn't hide**: a household's server holds its own people's
  keys, as it holds their chats. With one or two households it can work
  out the draw (the app says so); with more, it can sometimes work out
  who gives to one of its own people. The GFS and relays see only
  sealed data, sizes and timing.
- **Limits**: 3 to 30 people, wish lists up to 500 characters and 5
  updates after the draw. Couples need at least 4 people, with at most
  one couple per 3 people.

</details>

## Try it

Update to **Social Home 2026.11.1**, open **Apps › Store** and add
Chess. Invite someone from a paired household for a game this week. And
if your family does Secret Santa: start a gift draw, invite everyone,
and let Social Home keep the secret.
