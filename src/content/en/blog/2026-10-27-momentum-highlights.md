Highlights and Momentum are the two quick ways to share in Social Home.
A **highlight** is a few photos or short videos from your day, for the
people you pick. A **moment** is a short post that travels from your
household to your paired households and theirs. Both are meant to be
light. Both should also do exactly what they say.

Now both get a fresh look, like
[Spaces](/blog/2026-10-20-spaces-redesign/) last week, and both say who
sees what and for how long, before you share. It all ships in **Social
Home 2026.10.27**.

_All screens below show a sample household: the Bergers._

## Highlights: who's new, and who sees yours

The Highlights page now has two tabs, **Today** and **Archive**.

**Today** starts with your own highlight: how many frames, how many
people saw it, and who it's for. Below, one row per person, split into
**New** and **Seen**. Every row says when it was posted, how many frames
it has, and who it was for: Paired households, Household only, Just you
or Picked people. A highlight about to disappear says so, "ends in 2
hours".

The rings moved to **Home**, where they're useful: one card at the top
of the Feed with the people who have something new, and a line for

**Archive** is now a list of days, newest first, with a month picker

## One audience for the day

A highlight is still one per person per day, and now that's said out
loud. Your first frame of the day sets who sees it, starting from your
default in Settings. Add more frames later and the composer shows
"Goes to: …" with the time you chose it.

Changed your mind? **Change for all of today…** lets you narrow or widen
it, and tells you before anything happens: who will now see all of
today's frames, and who won't see today's highlight any more. People who
already watched it keep what they saw, and the sheet says that too. If
the highlight has a public link, you can remove it in the same step.

## Watching a highlight

The viewer has real buttons now, 44 pixels big and readable in light
and dark: **pause**, a menu, and close. Space or K pauses, the arrow keys
move between frames, and videos start muted so they actually play.
Opening a menu or "Seen by" over a frame pauses it.

- **Reactions are remembered.** Your emoji stays marked, tap it again to
  take it back, and the author is told who reacted.
- **Reply privately** sends the frame into a chat with the person who
  posted it.
- **For the author**, the menu can delete this one frame or the whole
  of today's highlight.
- When you reach the end, the viewer moves on to the next person with
  something new.

## Momentum: one list, and yours under Yours

Momentum now has three tabs: **Moments**, **Yours** and **Discover**.
Moments are flat cards like the household Feed, replies read as a
thread, and the **#** button filters by hashtag, in any language:
#Kürbisfest stays #Kürbisfest.

**Your own moments stay.** Other people see a moment for 24 hours, or
longer if they follow you. For you, every moment you post stays under
**Yours**, with
who sees it and until when. Ended ones read "Only you see this now."
Your household's admin can set how much space pictures and videos may
use per person (2 GB by default). Past that, the oldest pictures go
first and the text stays.

**Save someone else's moment** and it stays with you until its author
deletes it. When they do, your saved copy goes too: deleting still
means deleting. That's why Save only appears where a delete can reach
your copy, such as a moment from your own household or a directly
paired one.

**You decide how long followers keep your moments**: 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30
days, in **Settings › Privacy › Momentum**. Everyone else still sees
each one for 24 hours.

## Writing a moment: where it goes, and when

A moment goes to your paired households and on, up to 3 hops, and the
composer now says so in one line, "Paired households and
theirs, up to 3 hops · 24 h", with an ⓘ for the details.

- **The 15-minute limit shows before you write.** "You can post your
  next moment at 13:24", and you can write now; it stays there. The
  button reads "Post at 13:24" until then. Replies and reactions always
  go.
- **Public is a separate switch**, "Also public on" your GFS, and it's
  **off by default**, even after you sign up for public sharing. When
  it's on, the button reads **Post publicly**. Public is chosen when you
  write, not later.
- Closing with a draft asks **Discard this moment?** first.

Public sharing has moved to **Settings › Privacy › Public sharing**,
linked from Yours and from the composer.

## Discover and one Follow

**Discover** is now a Momentum tab, there as soon as your household is connected to a
[GFS (Global Federation Server)](/docs/glossary/#gfs), the relay that
helps households find each other.

The person sheet has one **Follow**, and it says what it does.
For someone from a paired household, you see their moments for as long
as they keep them, and it stays between your households. For someone
you only know from a GFS, it says "the GFS learns that you follow them".

## Reactions reach further, and counts add up

You can now react to moments from friends of friends, two or three
hops away, and from people you follow through a GFS. They're sealed for the
author's household: the households and the GFS that pass them along
can't see who reacted, with which emoji, or to what.

Only the author's household knows the full count, so it now sends the
totals back out, a few times while the moment is fresh and only when
they changed. Never one message per reaction, and never who reacted.
Until the first totals arrive, you see your own reaction without a
number.

<details class="tech">
<summary>Under the hood</summary>

- A far reaction is signed by your household (Ed25519), padded to 1 KB
  and sealed to the author household's key-wrap key (X25519). On the
  paired-household path it travels back the way the moment came, using
  a return token each relay added on the way out (AES-256-GCM under a
  key derived with HKDF from that relay's routing secret). Relays keep
  no state and learn only their own neighbours. A moment keeps up to 3
  return paths through different neighbours, and each reaction goes
  along 2 of them, so one relay that drops reactions can't silence
  them.
- Through a GFS, the same sealed reaction goes to the author's
  household only. The GFS sees that one household sent another
  something, nothing more.
- Totals: the 8 most used emoji plus the overall count, signed by the
  author's household. Checkpoints after posting are 10 minutes, 6 hours
  and 3 days for paired households, and 10 minutes, 3 hours and 24
  hours through the GFS, where they are sealed and padded to 640 bytes.
- Saves and late deletes: an author's delete keeps trying for 30 days,
  and a GFS keeps signed delete certificates (no content) for 30 days
  so a follower that reconnects still gets them.
- Widening today's highlight sends today's frames, at most 30, once, to
  each newly added household. Audience changes are limited to 6 an
  hour.
- No new keys were introduced.

</details>

## Protected accounts see their circle

A protected account, usually a child's, now sees moments only from its
own household and directly paired households. That's the same circle
its own moments reach. It has no Discover tab and no public switch, and
the page says so in plain words.

## Public links, with controls and honest messages

The [Social Home viewer](/blog/2026-10-24-pinned-viewer/) page that
opens a public highlight or moment link got a control bar: previous,
pause, next and mute. It also tells your friend whether the video comes
directly from your home or through the GFS, still encrypted.

When a link can't open, the message is plain. A highlight that can't be
reached always says "This isn't available right now", whether the link
was removed, has expired, or your home is offline, so the page never
tells a stranger whether you're at home. A damaged link says it's
incomplete. A moment link can say "no longer available", because it
never contacts your home at all.

## And a few more things

- Replies open in place on the moment page and can carry a photo or
  video.
- When something can't load, the page says so and offers **Try again**.
- Momentum and Highlights read right to left for Arabic and Hebrew, and
  names in any script keep their punctuation where it belongs.

## Try it

It's all in **Social Home 2026.10.27**. Post a highlight, then tap
**Change for all of today…** to see who would gain or lose it. Or open
Momentum › Yours and look back at what you've posted. It's still there.
The [Highlights](/docs/highlights/) and [Momentum](/docs/momentum/)
pages have the details.
