Organize is where a household keeps its everyday promises: call the
plumber, buy milk, don't forget the bins on Thursday. In
**Social Home 2026.10.2** all three tools got a full overhaul, from the
database up to the last pixel. We're really happy with it.

Tasks, Shopping and Stickies now share the same building blocks:
one quick-add bar, one loading skeleton, one error card with
**Retry**, one Undo toast. They look alike, behave alike, and every
control has a 44 px touch target on phones.

_All screens below show sample data._

## Tasks become a board

Tasks now live on a board with three fixed columns: **To do**,
**In progress** and **Done**. Each card shows its priority (None to
Urgent), up to three labels, one due chip (red once overdue) and who
is on it. The **List** view is still one click away if you prefer a
plain checklist.

Moving a card works the way your hands expect:

- **Mouse:** drag starts after 4 px. A ghost card and an insertion
  line show where it lands, and the board scrolls at the edges.
- **Touch:** press and hold for about 300 ms, so normal scrolling
  still works.
- **Keyboard:** Alt + ←/→ changes column, Alt + ↑/↓ reorders, and
  every move is announced to screen readers.

Under the hood, a move sends at most two requests: a status change
when the column changes, then a reorder that names the moved card.
Moves are queued per list, so two quick drags never collide. If
something fails, the card snaps back and tells you why.

Label colours are derived from the label's name, so they cost no extra
storage and nothing extra on the wire. Edit rights are clearer too:
the creator, an assignee or an admin can change a task. Everyone else
sees a 🔒 instead of a button that fails.

## A shopping list that keeps up

The list groups items by store, or shows them flat. Paste several
items at once and end a line with `@ Store` to file it in the right
place. Bought items move to the bottom, greyed out.

Deleting is now instant, with **Undo**. The trick: the delete only
goes to the server once the toast expires, so Undo never has to
re-create anything. **Clear all** removes exactly the items you saw
disappear, not the one you ticked a second later.

## Sticky notes, on every screen

The sticky wall is still your household's fridge door, and now it
works on phones too. Only the tape grip captures touches, so swiping
the board scrolls the page. Notes size themselves to the board.
Below about 760 px of board width they become a tidy grid in reading
order, and their stored positions stay untouched for the big screen.

Notes move by their grip or with the arrow keys (Shift moves five
times as far). Colours have names now: Yellow, Coral, Mint, Sky, Lilac
and Peach. Pick a dark custom colour and the text switches to light
ink by itself.

## Safer, and faster too

Reviewing the old code turned up a few things we fixed along the way:

- **Space tasks and sticky notes stay inside their space.** Every
  request is now checked against the space in the URL. A note or task
  from another space answers 404, exactly like one that doesn't exist.
- **A note's colour can't phone home.** Colours must be a plain
  `#RRGGBB` value now. Before, a crafted colour could make every
  member's browser load an outside image and leak their IP address.
- **Organize opens faster.** The "Tasks · N" count used to load every
  list. Now it comes from a single grouped query.

And everything is translated into English, German, Spanish, French
and Dutch.
