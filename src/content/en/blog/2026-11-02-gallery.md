The gallery is where your household keeps its photos and videos: the
summer holiday, the garden, Oma's birthday. In **Social Home 2026.11.2**
it is new from top to bottom.

Upload a whole holiday from your phone in one go. Find a photo by the
day it was taken, or by who added it. Keep gift ideas in an album only
you and your partner see. Share a copy with the family
[space](/docs/glossary/#space) so the grandparents get it too. And when
nobody is using the wall tablet, it shows your photos.

_The screens below show three sample families: the Müllers, the Okafors
and the Krämers._

## Your albums, and a memory

Open **Gallery** and your albums are there, the one someone added to
last at the top. An album with new photos from someone else says
**New**.

Some mornings there is a card at the top: **2 years ago today**, with
the photos from that day. Tap it to look back. You can hide it for
the day, leave an album out, or turn it off. It never sends a
notification.

Inside an album, photos are sorted by the day they were taken, under
month and day headings that say who added them. Newest first, or
oldest first if you'd rather read a holiday from the start. Social Home
remembers your choice for each album.

## A whole holiday in one go

Tap **Upload**, pick 200 photos, and put your phone down. Each file
shows how far it has got, and the screen stays on while they go up. If
the connection drops, the upload waits and carries on when you're back.
You can pause, cancel the rest, or try again on one that failed.

A photo that's already in the gallery isn't stored twice: it counts as
**already here**. On a phone, the camera button takes a photo straight
into the album. And you don't need an album first: photos go into
**Photos** until you sort them.

birthday song makes it. A video that has just arrived says how long it
will take to be ready.

## Swipe, zoom, and the details

Tap a photo to see it large. Swipe to the next one, pinch or double-tap
to zoom. Every album and every photo has its own link, so you can send
"look at this one" to someone at home.

**Info** shows the day it was taken, who added it and when. Social Home
removes the location from every photo and keeps only the day. If the
date is wrong, or the photo needs a caption, change it right there.

## Tidy up, with 30 days to change your mind

Tap **Select**, or press and hold a photo, then pick as many as you
like: move them to another album, download them, share them, add them
to your favourites or delete them.

Each photo can be deleted by whoever added it, or by an admin. If you
picked some that aren't yours, Social Home says so before anything
goes, and leaves those selected. Deleted photos go to the **trash** for
30 days, so a slip of the thumb is easy to undo.

When someone leaves the household, their photos stay where they are,
their shared albums pass to an admin, and the admin can give them a ZIP
of their photos to take along.

## Favourites and search, just for you

Tap ♡ on a photo you love. **Favourites** collects them from every
album in one place, and nobody else sees yours.

Looking for something? Search by album, caption, who added it or the
month: "Sommer", "by Lena", "July 2025". The gallery never looks at
faces, objects or places in your photos.

## Private albums for gift ideas

Albums are open to everyone at home. Make one **private** and only the
people you pick see it: not in their albums, not on the wall, no
notifications to anyone else. Good for gift ideas, or for the surprise
party.

An admin sees that a private album exists, with its owner and how many
photos it has, but not its name or its photos.

Children can have private albums too, and their guardian always sees
them. A child deletes only their own photos and shares only where they
may post anyway. An admin can switch adding photos off for children
for a while.

A private album keeps photos out of sight in the app. It is not
encryption: whoever runs your Social Home server can still read the
files on its disk. The app says so, right where you choose.

## Share a copy with the family

Oma is in the family space, not in your household. Select the photos,
tap **Share**, and choose where they go: your household feed, a space
(as a post or into one of its albums) or a chat.

Before you send, Social Home says who will see them: "A copy goes to
the space Familie Müller. The 3 households in it can see it." It's a
copy, so the photos stay in your album. Did someone else add some of
them? They'll see that you shared them.

A space has its own gallery, under **More › Gallery**, with the same
dates, viewer, search and selection. Photos from every household in the
space sit side by side. A caption, a date or a move to another album
reaches everyone in the space.

## One line, not eighty

When Lena adds a whole afternoon at the beach, you get one
notification, not one per photo: "Lena Müller added 12 photos to
Sommer 2026". If she adds more while it's unread, the count goes up.

## The wall tablet as a photo frame

When nobody touches the [household dashboard](/blog/2026-10-13-dashboard-and-zones/)
on the wall for a few minutes, it can show your photos, with the day
and the album in a corner. A tap brings the board back. It doesn't
run at night, and never while the wall is locked.

An admin turns it on under **Admin › Dashboard › Photo screensaver**,
sets after how many minutes it starts and picks the albums. Only
albums everyone at home sees can be picked: a private album never
shows up on the wall.

<details class="tech">
<summary>Under the hood</summary>

- **What's stored**: photos as WebP up to 2560 px, videos as VP9 WebM
  up to 1920 px, thumbnails as WebP up to 512 px. Originals are not
  kept. The location is removed; only the day taken is kept.
- **Uploads**: three files at a time, waiting on a lost connection or
  a busy server and sending the same file again. The screen stays on
  with the Wake Lock API where the browser has it. An exact duplicate
  (same SHA-256 of the uploaded bytes) stores nothing, or comes back
  from the trash.
- **Videos**: up to 5 minutes and 2 GiB in the household gallery;
  spaces, the feed and chats keep 60 seconds. Videos are converted one
  at a time; the "ready in about" estimate learns from your home's own
  speed.
- **Who may delete**: in the household, the person who added a photo
  or an admin. An album's owner deletes the album as a whole; its
  photos go to the trash and each can be restored by whoever added it.
  Protected accounts delete only their own. In a space, the space's
  roles decide, and a delete is final: a space has no trash.
- **Trash**: 30 days, then an hourly job removes it for good.
- **Private albums**: every read checks who may see the album, on the
  server: albums, search, favourites, notifications, the wall and the
  admin's export. It's an access rule, not encryption.
- **Search**: SQLite full-text search over captions, album names and
  descriptions, plus who added it and the month. No face, object or
  place recognition.
- **Favourites** stay in your home and are never sent to anyone, not
  even to the other households in a space.
- **Space galleries**: photos and edits travel encrypted home to home
  to the space's member households only. A caption, date or album
  change is one sealed update; each household checks that it
  came from whoever added the photo or a space owner, admin or
  moderator.
- **Shares** are copies of the stored files, sent the way any post or
  space photo is sent. Up to 50 at a time; a video over a minute is
  trimmed to its first minute, or left out, your choice.
- **Notifications**: uploads by the same person to the same album are
  grouped (90 seconds of quiet, at most 10 minutes). A push names who
  and the space, never the album.
- **Photo screensaver**: up to 30 photos a day, 20 seconds each with a
  2-second crossfade (a plain cut with reduced motion). The ESPHome wall
  panels get the same photos through the display API, drawn at the
  panel's size.

</details>

## Try it

Update to **Social Home 2026.11.2** and open **Gallery**. Upload last
summer's holiday in one go, make a private album for gift ideas, and
share your favourite with the family space. Then ask your admin to turn
on the photo screensaver.
