Most marketplaces put your stuff in front of strangers and take a
cut. Social Home's **Marketplace** does the opposite: listings
live inside a **space**, so only the households in that space —
your street, your building, your extended family — ever see them.
No platform fees, no boosted listings, no data sold to anyone.

You'll find it inside any space that has it enabled, alongside
the feed, chat, and calendar.

## Listing types

When you post an item you pick how you want to sell it:

- **Fixed price** — one price, first to take it wins.
- **Best offer** — buyers send what they'd pay; you choose.
- **Starting bid** — a floor price that offers build up from.
- **Negotiable** — an asking price that's openly up for a chat.
- **Auction** — a timed sale with a live countdown; highest bid
  when the clock runs out takes it.

Prices show in your chosen currency (euro by default). A listing
runs for **1, 3, 5, or 7 days**, then expires on its own — and
its status moves through active → sold / expired / cancelled so
nobody chases a thing that's already gone.

## It stays inside the space

A listing is scoped to the space it's posted in. It federates to
the other households in that space — and to nobody else. By
default it won't even announce itself in the space feed; flip on
the feed shout-out per listing if you want the extra visibility.

Because Marketplace rides the same household-to-household
federation as everything else, there's no central catalogue to
browse, no third party brokering the sale, and no fees. It's a
notice board for people who already know each other.
